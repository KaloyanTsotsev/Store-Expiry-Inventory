# StoreFlow: delivery article codes and expiry entry

The delivery row's `article_code` from the “Артикул” column is the source of `products.barcode` for this workflow. A code is kept as text: `0000123` and `123` are distinct. Document-level barcodes remain photo evidence only. No audit export is used as a data source.

## What changed

| Area | Finding and change | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OCR and matching | The OCR adapter already exposed the article code, but the old catalog workflow only matched supplier associations and left product barcodes empty. The new workflow checks the loaded catalog by exact barcode first. It reuses an existing product's name and ID even if OCR suggests a different name. The catalog is loaded once per opening or explicit refresh, not once per row. | `lib/services/paddle_document_adapter.dart`, `lib/services/product_matcher.dart`, `lib/services/product_catalog_service.dart` |
| Safe writes | The new employee-authenticated `finalize-article` route verifies the staged rows, checks for conflicts again, and writes a confirmed new product or fills a blank barcode in the same transaction as the existing V2 batch finalization. A blank barcode can be linked via a verified supplier association or explicit product selection in Details. Name-only matches are never assigned automatically. Existing nonempty conflicting barcodes are not replaced. No catalog record is deleted or renamed. Existing supplier associations and delivery links are kept. | `backend/catalog_v3/pb_hooks/catalog_v3.pb.js`, `backend/catalog_v3/pb_hooks/lib/catalog_v3.js`, `lib/services/delivery_finalization_service.dart`, `lib/services/delivery_row_status.dart`, `lib/screens/product_form_screen.dart` |
| OCR names and batch review | One delivery-document OCR pass still extracts the table; there is no second product-name OCR call. A recognized catalog product skips name review. New names with empty/placeholder text, replacement characters, obvious truncation, or mixed Latin/Cyrillic letters within a word are blocked until corrected. Plausible OCR errors still need employee review. The original OCR remains available. Existing products show their catalog identity and keep expiry, received quantity and optional lot editable. | `lib/models/delivery_draft.dart`, `lib/widgets/bulk_review_row.dart`, `lib/screens/delivery_editor_screen.dart`, `lib/screens/delivery_item_screen.dart` |
| Date input | Calendar or manual entry is available for known-product, inline delivery and delivery-detail forms. The parser accepts all six requested formats, interprets local dates day–month–year, maps two-digit years to 2000–2099, rejects impossible dates and previews alternate formats. Saving sends ISO date-only values. | `lib/models/delivery_draft.dart`, `lib/widgets/expiry_date_field.dart`, `lib/screens/known_product_batch_screen.dart` and the review forms above |
| Supplier and expiry checks | No supplier popup is required for a known product. Delivery details retain the optional inline supplier and remembered default. The handled-expiry action still updates only one batch, keeps its history and creates no waste movement. The old unsynced warning was caused by a local-only queue; the sending/retry flow stays in place, and overlapping dashboard refreshes now share one send attempt. | `lib/widgets/expiry_dashboard.dart`; see also [the expiry workflow report](expiry-workflow-fixes.md) for the existing hook, queue and supplier files |

## Verification and rollout

1. On a test PocketBase with the updated Catalog V3 hooks and enabled V2/Catalog delivery capabilities, import a document with an article such as `0000123`. Confirm the row and finalize. Check the exact string in `products.barcode`. Import another date for that article and verify two batches with the same product ID. A known product can also be entered directly with “Добави срок по баркод” without OCR or supplier entry.
2. Select an existing product with a blank barcode in the row's Details. Confirm and finalize; check that only the barcode and catalog audit/revision change. Try a conflicting barcode or a corrupted new name and verify no product, batch or movement is committed.
3. Open the date calendar and choose a date. Replace it manually with `25/11/26`; check the preview `25.11.2026`. Try all listed formats, an invalid date such as `31.02.2026`, and a corrected OCR suggestion. Confirm the intended normalized date in PocketBase.
4. Confirm “Премахни от списъка” while offline, restore connectivity and refresh. The entry should stay visible until acknowledged, then disappear on both phones after refresh. The other expiry date, catalog product and stock history should remain unchanged.

This change uses the existing text field, revision/audit fields and V2 finalization, with no new migration. The app uses the new route only when the server advertises `delivery_article_barcode`. Older clients and already-staged legacy requests keep their prior contract. Deploy the updated client and the two updated Catalog V3 hook files together after verification; include the existing expiry-handling hooks if they were not deployed previously. No APK build or production change was performed.
